Water + wastewaterCCCS / WTR-08

Treatment-plant execution

Upgrade the process without losing sight of plant operations.

Water and wastewater projects are constructed inside facilities that must continue treating, pumping, disinfecting, and distributing water throughout the work. Routing, material selection, corrosion exposure, bypass requirements, shutdown windows, temporary service, testing, and operator access must be resolved as one operating plan.

We develop fabrication and installation sequences around the plant’s process flow, equipment interfaces, isolation points, access limitations, and approved cutover windows. The result is a field-ready piping package that supports safe construction, documented testing, and a controlled return to service.

Project principleProtect treatment capacity while building the plant’s next phase.
