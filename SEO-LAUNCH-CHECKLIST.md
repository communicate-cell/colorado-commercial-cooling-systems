# SEO launch checklist

This checklist covers the account-level work that begins after the website is live at `https://cocommercialcooling.com/`. The code package already includes unique page titles and descriptions, canonical URLs, Organization structured data, `robots.txt`, and `sitemap.xml`.

## 1. Create or claim the Google Business Profile

Only create a separate profile for Colorado Commercial Cooling Systems if the company is eligible: it must make in-person contact with customers and operate as a genuine customer-facing business, not only as a brand or lead-generation name.

1. Sign in with a company-controlled Google Account. A Google Account can use the company email; it does not have to use a Gmail address.
2. Visit `https://business.google.com/add` and first search for the exact company name, **Colorado Commercial Cooling Systems**, to avoid creating a duplicate.
3. If no profile exists, select **Add your business to Google**.
4. Use the real company name without extra keywords.
5. Select the most accurate primary category offered by Google, likely **HVAC contractor**. Add other accurate categories only if they describe services actually provided.
6. Enter the real operating address for verification. If customers do not visit that location, configure the company as a service-area business and hide the street address from the public profile.
7. Add only the cities or regions the team genuinely serves. Do not use a false address or create separate profiles for cities without staffed locations.
8. Add the authoritative phone number, **(720) 773-9796**, and website, `https://cocommercialcooling.com/`.
9. Complete the verification method Google offers. Google decides whether this is video, phone, email, live video, or mail.
10. After verification, add the approved logo, real project photographs, service descriptions, business hours, and the project-inquiry URL, `https://cocommercialcooling.com/contact.html`.
11. Keep the name, phone, website, and service information consistent with the website and other reputable company listings.

## 2. Connect Google Search Console

1. Visit `https://search.google.com/search-console/` and sign in with a company-controlled Google Account.
2. Select **Add property**, then choose **Domain**.
3. Enter `cocommercialcooling.com` without `https://` or a path.
4. Copy the TXT verification record supplied by Google.
5. Sign in to the company that manages the domain's DNS records. Add the TXT record exactly as Google provides it; do not remove existing MX, SPF, DKIM, or other email-related records.
6. Return to Search Console and select **Verify**. DNS changes can take time to become visible.
7. Open **Sitemaps**, enter `sitemap.xml`, and submit it.
8. Use **URL Inspection** to test the home page and the Data Centers, Chiller Systems, Process Piping, Water + Wastewater, and Contact pages. Select **Request indexing** when the live test succeeds.
9. Check the **Pages** report for indexing problems and the **Performance** report for search terms, clicks, impressions, and average position.

## 3. Confirm the deployment details

1. In Netlify, make `cocommercialcooling.com` the primary domain.
2. Redirect the `www` version to the primary non-`www` version, and confirm every page uses HTTPS.
3. Open `https://cocommercialcooling.com/robots.txt` and confirm it loads.
4. Open `https://cocommercialcooling.com/sitemap.xml` and confirm it lists every public page.
5. Confirm that `https://cocommercialcooling.com/thank-you.html` is not included in the sitemap. It is intentionally marked `noindex`.
6. Test every navigation link and submit a real test inquiry through the contact form.

## 4. Earn relevant links to the site

A backlink is a link to this website from another website. Relevant links help Google discover the company and understand that established organizations recognize it. Quality and relevance matter more than the number of links.

Good first opportunities include:

- A short partnership announcement on the Commercial Welding and Advanced Refrigeration websites linking to the Colorado Commercial Cooling Systems home page.
- Accurate company profiles in trade-association, manufacturer, supplier, and contractor directories in which the company genuinely participates.
- General-contractor or engineering-partner pages that identify the company on a completed project, when the owner and contract permit it.
- Municipal or institutional vendor directories that publish a company profile and website link.
- Local chamber or construction-industry membership profiles.

Avoid buying bulk links, joining unrelated directories, exchanging large numbers of links, or placing keyword-heavy links on low-quality sites. Those tactics can create spam signals instead of authority.

## 5. Maintain search visibility

- Review Search Console monthly during the first six months.
- Keep Google Business Profile hours, phone, photos, and services current.
- Ask real customers for honest Google reviews after successful work; never buy or script reviews.
- Update the sitemap dates only when page content changes substantially.
- Add project pages later when approved photographs and publishable technical details become available.
