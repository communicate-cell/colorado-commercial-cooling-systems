Why we formed

A partnership built to remove the weak point: the handoff.

Cooling plants depend on linked decisions involving performance, equipment selection, service access, routing, supports, fabrication, controls, testing, and startup. When those decisions are made separately, conflicts tend to surface during procurement or installation—when the available solutions are more expensive and disruptive.

Colorado Commercial Cooling Systems brings equipment and piping knowledge into the project at the beginning, when decisions about selection, access, routing, procurement, fabrication, installation, and turnover can still be coordinated effectively.
