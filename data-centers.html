Data center focus

Built around redundancy, phasing, and predictable turnover.

Data center chiller installation is not ordinary mechanical construction. Every decision must account for system topology, future capacity, isolation, active operations, quality records, controls integration, and the turnover sequence.

For mission-critical cooling systems, equipment capacity, chilled-water piping, isolation boundaries, controls interfaces, testing, and commissioning milestones are coordinated around the facility’s uptime requirements and expansion plan.
