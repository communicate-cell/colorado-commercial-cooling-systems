## How the terms were implemented without changing visible page content

- Each indexable page has a distinct, descriptive title and meta description aligned with its service intent.
- The home page identifies the exact company name and core services in organization and website structured data.
- Service pages include accurate service structured data that matches the services already visible on those pages.
- Canonical URLs remain in place, and the XML sitemap carries the current modification date.
- Indexable pages explicitly permit normal crawling and large image previews.
- Draft and form-confirmation pages remain excluded from search results.
- No `meta keywords` tag, invisible paragraph, off-screen keyword list, or repeated keyword grid was added.
