Public-sector delivery

Technical execution with public accountability.

Public works mechanical construction brings its own requirements: open procurement, defined submittal workflows, operating-facility constraints, public schedules, documentation, and long-term maintainability.

For public facility chiller replacement and new institutional cooling systems, we organize the equipment, piping, controls interfaces, testing, and commissioning around those realities. Project teams receive clearer scope ownership and a coordinated path from equipment approval through system turnover.

Project principleDeliver a maintainable public asset—not just a completed installation.
